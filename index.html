Exterior

Exterior Protection

  • Thorough exterior prewash
  • Meticulous hand wash
  • Wheel and tire cleaning
  • Bug and road grime removal
  • Ceramic spray protection
  • Professional tire dressing

Your car's exterior takes a beating from sun, rain, bird droppings, and road salt. We begin with a thorough prewash to loosen and safely remove surface contaminants before contact washing. The vehicle is then carefully hand washed and dried before the paint is finished with a ceramic spray protectant. Wheels and tires are cleaned separately and finished with a professional tire dressing for a complete exterior appearance.

Ideal for maintaining a clean, glossy exterior, refreshing a vehicle that has become dull or weathered, preparing a vehicle for sale, or protecting a recently purchased vehicle.

Interior

Interior Restoration

  • Deep stain removal from upholstery and carpets
  • Professional seat extraction
  • Complete carpet cleaning and shampooing
  • All-surface cleaning including dashboards, vents, and cup holders
  • Thorough pet hair removal from every crevice
  • Leather treatment

The process involves cleaning every surface of the interior, starting with a thorough removal of built-up dirt, dust, and debris. Surfaces are treated with the appropriate cleaning solution to break down grime and restore a clean appearance. Once cleaned, interior surfaces are finished with a quality dressing to enhance their appearance while leaving behind a clean, refined finish.

Ideal for vehicles with accumulated dirt, stains, pet hair, heavily used interiors, or anyone looking to restore a cleaner and more refreshed cabin.
